Associate Editor: 2
Comments to the Author:
This paper studies the stability of the SGD trajectory. While this analysis provides some insights on the behavior of SGD, there are some issues in the submission. For example, the main contributions study the norm of SGD iterations, and both reviewers do not see how this is related to the stability of SGD. There is not a sufficient justification of this norm on stability. Furthermore, there are some missing discussions on the existing studies on the norm control of SGD iterations. The analyses also impose restrictive assumptions on the correlation of gradients, which may not hold in practice. I hope these comments would be useful for the authors to improve their paper.
