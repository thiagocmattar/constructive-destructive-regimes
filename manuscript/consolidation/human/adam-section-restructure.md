In full batch, using the fundamental theorem of calculus,

\\[ g_t-g\_{t-1} = -\eta\int_0^1 H(\theta\_{t-2}-s\eta d\_{t-1})d\_{t-1}\\,ds. \\]

### The important interpretation

The equation separates two directions:

- \\(d\_{t-1}\\) determines the direction along which the parameters move.
- \\(g\_{t-1}\\) determines the direction onto which the resulting gradient change is projected.

Thus, your diagnostic measures the projected gradient response along the trajectory actually generated by the optimizer.

For SGD, \\(d\_{t-1}=g\_{t-1}\\), so this becomes the usual directional curvature (Rayleigh quotient, averaged along the step). For Adam, the expression is a mixed Hessian projection. But its computation remains identical.

I would therefore preserve your diagnostic and define constructive/destructive regimes under Adam as raw-gradient alignment regimes, rather than directly as reinforcement or cancellation of parameter updates.
