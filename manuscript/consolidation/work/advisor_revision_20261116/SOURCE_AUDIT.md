# Primary-source audit for the advisor revision

Checked 16 November 2026. This audit supports the wording of the existing literature comparisons; it does not establish priority for a broad idea. Bibliography entries were retained.

| Source | Supported comparison and editorial consequence |
|---|---|
| [Barzilai and Borwein (1988)](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~swright/726/handouts/barzilai-borwein.pdf), [publisher record](https://academic.oup.com/imajna/article-abstract/8/1/141/802460) | BB1 uses the parameter-difference squared norm divided by its inner product with the gradient difference. The paper's step-direction secant quantity is its reciprocal when defined. Abstract/introduction/related work assign the contribution to the framework and investigation, not invention of this secant construction. |
| [Central Flows, Section 7.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.24206) | The authors describe the remaining gap between loss-level dynamics and the mechanistic role of individual network layers. The introduction uses that specific architectural question; it does not claim prior work ignores representations. |
| [Bai et al., Sections 4.3, Appendix C, and Appendix G.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.04805v2), [ICML 2026 record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/bai26o.html) | Their Adam analysis concerns the adaptive preconditioner and a gradient-directional preconditioned-curvature diagnostic. Its implementation uses one HVP in the chosen direction, rather than constructing the Hessian. Monitoring cost is discussed. Our raw-gradient response is a different quantity, not a direct substitute for their measurement. |
| [Lee and Lee, Section 6.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.18415v2) | Their diagnostic combines the current gradient, applied update direction, and observed loss change. It is a completed-step loss balance without HVPs. Our secant measurement also describes a completed step, but uses gradient differences. No exclusive claim to inexpensive diagnostics is made. |
| [Kosson et al., Section 6 and Appendix B.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.23922) | Representation-change correction during warmup is existing work. Their relative correction and running state differ from the present absolute per-unit cap with an eight-update frozen calibration prefix. The manuscript presents the cap as an intervention test, not the first representation-based optimizer control. |
| [Brock et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/brock21a.html), [Benzing](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/benzing22a.html) | Existing comparisons to unit-wise gradient/weight scaling and neuron-output optimization are retained. The experiment does not benchmark or rank these methods. |
| [Zhu et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/zhu24h.html) | Catapult/feature-learning work motivates examining outcomes beyond fluctuation suppression. It does not prove that the Adam oscillations measured here are necessary for learning. |

The earlier [Section II audit](../section2_framing_20261115/OBSERVATIONS.md) records the retained convergence-diagnostic, stochastic-stability, path-length, and representation-similarity comparisons. No new biological factual claim was added: activity regulation appears only as a possible analogy requiring its own mechanism and validation. No neuron inactivity percentage is reported.
