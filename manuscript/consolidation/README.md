## Working agreement

and publication figures have not been edited. No experiment or paid computation
was launched. This plan does not authorize its proposed experiments.

We will revise the argument, scope, evidence choices, and task cards through as
many consolidation iterations as needed. Manuscript editing starts only when
the author is satisfied with the modification plan and asks to apply it.
Task IDs here are planning identifiers, not allocated hypothesis/run/analysis IDs.

planning documents outside that directory. Scientific outputs continue to belong
to their owning analyses/runs, with links from here rather than duplicate data.
