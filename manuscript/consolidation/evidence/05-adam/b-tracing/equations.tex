\subsection*{V-B. Fixed-probe representation motion}
For a fixed ordered probe, $A_s^\ell$ retains all sample/token/spatial/feature
coordinates, with $P_\ell$ scalar entries. Define outgoing motion
\begin{equation}
\delta_s^\ell=\operatorname{vec}(A_{s+1}^\ell-A_s^\ell),\qquad
\rho_s^\ell=\frac{\langle\delta_s^\ell,\delta_{s-1}^\ell\rangle}
{\|\delta_s^\ell\|\|\delta_{s-1}^\ell\|},\qquad
a_s^\ell=\frac{\|\delta_s^\ell\|}{\sqrt{P_\ell}} .
\label{consol:trace-motion}
\end{equation}
A zero displacement has undefined cosine. This outgoing response is measured
after update $s\to s+1$, whereas raw-gradient $\rho_s$ is available before it.
Raw-gradient streams are not differenced.

For $s=a,\ldots,b$, $T=b-a+1$:
\begin{equation}
D_\ell=
\begin{bmatrix}(\delta_a^\ell)^\top\\ \vdots\\(\delta_b^\ell)^\top\end{bmatrix}
\in\mathbb R^{T\times P_\ell},\qquad
K_\ell=D_\ell D_\ell^\top,\qquad
D_\ell=U_\ell\Sigma_\ell V_\ell^\top .
\label{consol:trace-svd}
\end{equation}
Use uncentered, unnormalized rows in the primary fit.
With $q=\min(T,P_\ell)$, thin factors have sizes
$U:T\times q$, $\Sigma:q\times q$, $V:P_\ell\times q$.
\begin{equation}
\alpha_{s,k}^\ell=\sigma_{\ell,k}U_{\ell,sk},\qquad
E_k^\ell=\frac{\sum_{j\leq k}\sigma_{\ell,j}^2}{\sum_j\sigma_{\ell,j}^2},
\qquad
O_k(\ell,F)=\frac{\|U_{\ell,k}^\top U_{F,k}\|_F^2}{k}.
\label{consol:trace-summaries}
\end{equation}
Only use $k$ within both numerical ranks. Zero total energy makes energy fractions
undefined. The Gram recovers temporal coefficients without materializing $V$.
Temporal overlap is not equality of neuron directions.

Magnitude controls use $K_{\rm orth}=\operatorname{diag}(\operatorname{diag}K)$ and
$K_{\rm unit}=B^{-1/2}KB^{-1/2}$, $B=\operatorname{diag}(\operatorname{diag}K)$,
excluding zero rows from normalization. The first preserves step magnitudes;
the second removes their weighting. They are descriptive references, not null tests.
For any temporal permutation matrix $P$, $PKP^\top$ has the same eigenvalues:
spectral concentration alone does not establish oscillation.

For cross-entropy, per-example/token correct-label log probabilities $\ell_{s,i}$
satisfy $L_s^{\rm probe}=-N_{\rm probe}^{-1}\sum_i\ell_{s,i}$ under the recorded
equal-image or valid-token weighting. They connect output motion to probe loss.
