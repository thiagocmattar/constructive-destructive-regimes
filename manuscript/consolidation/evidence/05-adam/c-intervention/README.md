# V-C. Intervention comparisons

- **Paper claim:** A matched comparison of LR, beta2, raw-gradient clipping and local affine-displacement caps separates excursion burden, learning progress and functional motion.
- **Boundary:** One initialization/model and unequal effective strengths. Retain all endpoints and held-out tradeoffs; local displacement bounds do not imply global stability, generalization or universal superiority.
- **Authority:** the [single paper plan](../../../PLAN.md) governs selection and closure.
- **Presentation:** Consolidation-only V-C draft complete: three IEEE-style pages with the main tradeoff and q50 heatmaps (24/25), a compact 12-row table, Algorithm 2 and precise captions. Ordinary figure labels are 9pt; full values and final-size rendering are verified. The original 17-23 PDFs retain supporting trajectories, held-out outcomes, activity and detailed tables. Full-paper integration and final numbering remain open; extra seeds/grid remain deferred.
- **Current experiment record:** [matched setup, execution status, ETC/costs and outputs](EXPERIMENTAL_PLAN.md). Completed and deferred work are distinguished there.
