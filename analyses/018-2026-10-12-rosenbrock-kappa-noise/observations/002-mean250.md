# 002: Separate 250-pair trailing-mean overlay

The user requested a longer trailing mean for the noise/LR overlay while
preserving the original figure. This is a visualization sensitivity check
within H017; the scientific paths and per-pair quantities are unchanged.

Sources, conditions, formula, noise pairing and interpretation limits are
those of [observation001](001-noise-overlay.md) and [METHOD.md](../METHOD.md).
All15 immutable Runs038-052 are reused, with sigma0/0.01/0.1/1/10,
three constant LRs, one PCG64 seed1234, start(0,1),2000AdamW updates,
betas(0.9,0.95), numerical epsilon1e-8, weight decay0.1 and float64.
Exact source/derived histories are hashed in [results.json](../results.json).

Reduction: compute the complete finite trailing arithmetic mean of250
unsmoothed adjacent-pair values for each plotted metric, rather than applying
another smoother to the existing100-pair mean. The first full mean is t=251;
all1,750 means at t251..2000 are displayed on each of30 curves, for52,500
finite plotted points. No partial windows, clipping, thinning or seed averaging.
The first250 update slots are undefined. The new plot's Y bounds cover all
finite values using the original scale policy: common linear Y on the left,
linear Y range per noise row on the right. No trajectory is rerun.

Caption: **Rosenbrock gradient-identity diagnostics with250-pair trailing means.**
Five rows show sigma0,0.01,0.1,1,10. Three LR overlays per panel use blue solid
for0.01, purple short dashed for0.0031622776601683794, and orange long dashed
for0.001. Left: q_t=1-rho_t*norm(g_t)/norm(g_previous). Right:
eta*kappa_proxy=q_t+epsilon_t, where epsilon_t is the realized normalized
gradient-noise difference, not the optimizer's numerical epsilon. All250-pair
means are shown. Left Y is shared; right Y ranges vary by row. The original
100-pair rendering is preserved. One paired seed; SGD-derived proxy on AdamW.

The longer mean suppresses short-scale fluctuations and spreads transitions
over a longer interval. It retains the distinction between the gradient-only
level near1 at high noise and the much smaller noise-corrected quantity.
This is a different reduction of the same realization, not a new replication
or stronger statistical evidence. Adjacent rolling windows remain dependent;
the correction still does not remove AdamW update mismatch or path changes.
No ensemble law, curvature-estimator validation or stability threshold follows.
