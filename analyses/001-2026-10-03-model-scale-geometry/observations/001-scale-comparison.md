# Observation 001: raw and adaptive geometry versus scale

H002 asks whether adjacent cosine and cumulative A, B, and ratios behave
predictably with model size. The user also explicitly requests both raw gradients
and AdamW directions, and their individual norms. No sign, slope, or functional
form was specified before these runs; this is a descriptive scale comparison.

Sources: Run002 attempt02 (14M), Run003 attempt01 (31M), Run004 attempt01 (70M).
Each completed 712 updates and consumed all 1,491,711,416 MiniPile input tokens
and 1,490,983,041 supervised targets once, in 728,375 packed sequences. Full
validation covers all 500 fixed documents, 693,668 inputs and 693,329 targets,
including its short tail. All three source attempts passed strict local artifact
acceptance and independent scientific reconstruction before cloud removal.
Exact paths/configs/event/summary hashes and matched contract are in ../results.json.
All 2,136 per-step reductions, including undefined first cosines and partial tails,
are retained in ../figure_data.csv. Generated by ../01_compare.py and ../02_record.py.

Matched factors: seed 1234, random SmallInit/WangInit recipe (not pretrained weights),
cache and permutation bytes, tokenizer, sequence length 2048, full-pass tokens,
712 updates, effective batch 1024/microbatch 4, token-weighted CE, AdamW betas
(0.9,0.95)/epsilon1e-8/decay0.1, bias/LayerNorm decay exclusions, clip1,
peakLR1e-3/minLR1e-4/warmup1%, FP16 dynamic scaling, FP32 weights/moments,
FP64 cumulative vectors/reductions and adaptive directions. Actual model counts
are below. Six layers; width128/256/512 and heads4/8/8 vary. Initialization
recipe matches, while shapes, tensor values and width-dependent standard deviations
vary. CPU execution-platform initial hashes were pinned before optimizer steps.
The 14M source is the paired Run002 trajectory, not Run001's separate replay.

Raw g is captured after accumulation/unscaling, before clipping. Adaptive d is
the bias-corrected preconditioned direction from actual updated FP32 moments of
the clipped gradient, evaluated in FP64. It excludes LR scaling, weight decay,
and parameter-subtraction rounding. A_t=sum_{i<=t}||x_i||, B_t=||sum_{i<=t}x_i||,
R_t=B_t/A_t; cosine compares current and previous vectors in the same full-model
parameter order. First cosine is undefined. Zero denominators are null with reason.

Observed ordering at this seed: raw median cosine falls from 0.007220 to −0.086252
to −0.160566, and adaptive median cosine from 0.966250 to 0.952865 to 0.931795.
Raw negative-pair fractions rise from 49.23% to 56.96% to 67.65%; adaptive negative
pairs remain 0/711 at every size. Final R declines for both streams: raw
0.337132→0.330751→0.309380, adaptive 0.704780→0.665558→0.609436.
Thus local adaptive alignment remains strongly positive, while its global
resultant is a smaller fraction of A at larger size. Adaptive A−B gaps are
29.52%,33.44%,39.06% of A, so A does not converge to B in this size comparison.

Absolute cumulative scales move in opposite directions: raw A and B decrease
across these sizes, whereas adaptive A and B increase. Adaptive median norm
increases 1250.01→1627.65→2132.93. Median raw norm is not monotonic
(0.409031→0.386883→0.394048). The median paired adaptive/raw norm ratio
increases 3175.95→4209.50→5382.23, for unscaled directions. Those are not
actual parameter-step magnitudes.

The ordering is not uniform at every step or in every phase. Raw cosine is more
positive in the last quarter at smaller size: medians 0.346110,0.163892,0.010837
for 14M,31M,70M. In the second quarter, however, raw medians are
−0.287772,−0.321731,−0.282898; these do not have the full-pass ordering.
The early adaptive R curves also cross, and their minima are not monotonic
with size. A single size-dependent scalar does not summarize the whole evolution.
Fixed validation loss decreases 5.182392→4.611768→4.063201 while final R
also decreases; these observations alone do not show that cancellation harms
optimization, nor establish a causal relationship with validation loss.

Evolution is summarized in four fixed quarters, not inferred regime boundaries.
Cosines are the original step records, including the pair across a quarter boundary;
end-R uses the whole trajectory prefix, not a quarter-only accumulator.

Interpretation limits: one seed per size supplies no estimate of seed variation.
Steps are correlated, not independent replications. Three sizes and no held-out
size cannot establish predictive scaling, a power law, or out-of-sample behavior.
Architecture (including attention-head configuration) co-varies with count.
Absolute A/B/norms depend on dimension, parameterization, and the learned trajectory;
their change cannot be attributed to dimensionality alone. Adaptive/raw norms
combine clipping, averaging, and adaptive rescaling; they do not isolate each.
Positive adjacent cosines do not imply A≈B over a long trajectory. R<1 can arise
from non-collinearity even without negative pairs. No stability, causal learning
benefit, per-sample decomposition, full SGD identity, or generalization across
seeds is established. Validation loss is separately measured, not inferred from R.
H002's predictability claim remains inconclusive; any coherent ordering observed
here is a candidate trend for a future replicated/held-out comparison.
