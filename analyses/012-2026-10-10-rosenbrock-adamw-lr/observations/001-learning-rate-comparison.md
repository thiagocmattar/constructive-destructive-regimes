Reduction: all 2,001 positions and 2,000 transitions per condition, both
coordinates, float64. Loss is the original Rosenbrock objective after each

incorporating g_t, excluding decay. Decay directly shrinks both coordinates
by 1-LR*0.1 before the adaptive descent step. Learning rates never change.
Norms are Euclidean; A=sum of vector norms, B=norm of the vector sum, B/A the
prefix ratio. Prefixes start at update 1, never restart or use local windows.

clipping, thinning, weighting or uncertainty bands are introduced. On the

Result: the midpoint has the lowest final objective in this new cohort. Both
larger LRs approach the optimum's neighborhood with ongoing raw-gradient
sign reversals, mostly or entirely positive momentum adjacent cosine and
nonzero objective floors. The last-1,000 loss ranges are [0.000926788,
0.001067019] at 1e-2 and [0.000105260,0.000266370] at the midpoint. Their
final gradient norms are 0.483408 and 0.147387. LR 1e-3 progresses more slowly,
with positive adjacent cosines and no sampled loss increases during this
horizon. None reaches the joint loss/gradient threshold or sustains it in
the final 100 positions. Positive momentum alignment is not stationarity.

Matched endpoint comparisons: at 1e-2, adding decay changes final loss from
0.000141348339 to 0.00104246819, while negative momentum pairs fall from
65.3327% to 0.4002% and its late median changes from approximately -1 to
+0.632284. At 1e-3, final loss falls from 0.325412631 to 0.234111865; momentum
pairs stay positive in both conditions. Thus decay's finite-horizon objective
response differs across the endpoints, and more positive momentum alignment
does not uniformly order objective outcomes. These statistics compare the
unsmoothed recorded histories. The current Analysis012 figure shows rolling
cosine means, whereas Analysis011 still shows individual pairs; compare the
underlying histories or apply the same reduction for direct curve comparisons.

Uncertainty/caveats: one deterministic start/function/beta2 gives no sampling
error or seed uncertainty. LR scales both the adaptive step and the decay
step. Decay changes subsequent positions and gradients, so the cosine is
not an independently manipulated cause. The known optimum (1,1) is nonzero,
and shrinkage pulls toward the origin; decoupled AdamW is not an ordinary L2
gradient added to f. Final low loss or a ratio plateau cannot establish
convergence, stability, information destruction, a universal optimizer/LR
ranking or transfer to LLMs. The midpoint lacks a no-decay causal comparison.

reviewed for legends, all nine panels, labels and complete landscape coverage.
