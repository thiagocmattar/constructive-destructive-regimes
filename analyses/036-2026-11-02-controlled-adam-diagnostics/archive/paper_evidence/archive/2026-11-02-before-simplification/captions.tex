\paragraph{Compact overview.}
Controlled full-batch Adam from one frozen initialization per case, with three
constant rates and 1,024 updates each. Rows show training loss (log scale),
raw-gradient projected change, consecutive raw-gradient cosine, and norms
divided by their own value at state 0. Solid/dashed norm curves denote gradient/
stored first moment. Loss is unsmoothed. The other prominent curves are complete
eight-observation trailing arithmetic means, right aligned; faint projected-change
curves retain every raw observation. Norms are averaged in linear space before
log display. Horizontal state$t$ counts completed updates; the displayed gradient
and updated moment are available before displacement$t+1$. The reference at 2
is not an identified Adam stability boundary. Temporal analyses use raw values.

\paragraph{Micro views.}
Unsmoothed views of the completed episode with the largest relative loss peak
at each case's highest tested rate. Each crop starts 10 states before the peak
and ends at the first subsequent loss below its starting value. Dotted verticals
mark the 10\% episode onset, dashed verticals the loss maximum; dotted horizontals
in the loss row mark crop-start loss. Gradient and stored-momentum norms are
divided by their respective crop-start values, comparing proportional changes
rather than absolute magnitudes. Every point is retained; all diagnostics use
their parameter-state availability clock. The examples illustrate the sequence;
the census and forecasting analyses include other episodes and non-spike states.

\paragraph{Repeated episode onsets.}
All 27 large observed episodes at the highest rates:17 MLP,3 ResNet-32 and 7
Transformer. Time 0 is the first loss at least 10\% above the preceding 16-state
minimum, not the peak. Thin curves show individual events; thick curves are
pointwise medians. Loss is divided by the frozen episode baseline; each norm
is divided by its own value at relative state-8. No temporal smoothing or
independent-event confidence band is used. Both unfinished recoveries are
retained; every displayed state has data. Five MLP windows overlap the end of
a previous episode. Selecting large episodes conditions on the future loss;
this figure describes temporal recurrence and does not estimate alarm precision.

\paragraph{Forward precision-recall.}
Primary predictions evaluated in five later time blocks, shown within each
case across all three rates. Blue uses loss history, training age and arm;
orange adds continuous raw-gradient projected change. Curves use exactly the
same eligible decision states for both models; dotted lines show positive
prevalence. Average precision is shown in each legend. Case subsets contain
1,112/1,751/1,671 states and 319/66/110 positives for MLP/ResNet/Transformer.
These are subsets of pooled-model predictions, not separately fitted case
models. Curves are unsmoothed, and no test operating point is selected.

\paragraph{Episode table.}
Loss-only episode census across all nine arms. Large denotes an observed
peak at least 50\% above its frozen baseline. Cross denotes a fresh crossing
of projected change from at most 2 to above 2. Warnings occur 1--8 states before
10\% onset while loss is less than 5\% above its own recent minimum. Entries
are episodes with a warning over eligible episodes of that severity; every
episode remains in the denominator, including misses and those without an
eligible warning state. Lead is median[min,max] among hits. Gradient/momentum
columns require greater than 20\% one-state norm growth. Cens. counts unfinished
recoveries. A dash means no event or undefined lead, not zero warning precision.
Counts describe dependent recurrences, not independent replications.

\paragraph{Prediction table.}
Incremental prediction on identical forward-test decisions. The target is a
loss rise of at least 10\% relative to current loss within 8 updates; eligibility
restricts current loss to less than 5\% above its trailing minimum. AP is average
precision (higher is better); Brier is mean squared probability error (lower is
better). Added means the same loss/age/arm model plus continuous projected
change. Differences compare models within a row. Case rows subset pooled fits;
pooled scores weight decision states, not cases or independent episodes equally.
Five expanding-prefix fits use a 24-state exclusion gap and training-only scaling.
Temporal dependence and exploratory selection preclude an IID significance claim.
