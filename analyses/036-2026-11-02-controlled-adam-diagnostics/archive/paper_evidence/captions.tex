\paragraph{Overview.}
Controlled full-batch Adam at three constant rates per case, from one frozen
initialization each. Rows show training loss (log scale), raw-gradient projected
change and consecutive raw-gradient cosine. Loss is unsmoothed; prominent
non-loss curves are complete 16-observation trailing arithmetic means,
right aligned, without raw overlays. The loss axis uses ordinary numeric
tick labels on a logarithmic scale.
Panels(d)--(f) intentionally restrict the displayed range to$[-0.1,2.2]$;
values outside this range are retained unchanged in the data. The horizontal
coordinate counts completed updates and aligns each diagnostic to the state
where it is available. The reference at two is not an Adam stability boundary.

\paragraph{Spikes.}
Unsmoothed views of the completed episode with the largest relative loss peak
at each case's highest tested rate. Crops begin 10 states before their peak and
end at the first later loss below the starting value. Dotted verticals mark
10\% episode onset, dashed verticals the peak; dotted loss horizontals mark
the crop-start value. Gradient and stored-momentum norms are divided by their
own crop-start values. Every observation is retained. These loss-selected
examples illustrate local ordering; the recurrence table includes other
episodes and misses.

\paragraph{Recurrence table.}
Descriptive loss-only episode census across all nine arms. Large means an
observed peak at least 50\% above the frozen episode baseline. Earlier crossing
means projected change moves from at most two to above two,1--8 updates before
10\% onset while loss remains less than 5\% above its own recent minimum.
Fractions retain all episodes of the indicated severity, including misses.
Lead is median[min,max] among large episodes with a qualifying crossing;
a dash means no episode or undefined lead. One highest-rate MLP and one
highest-rate Transformer recovery are unfinished. Repeated episodes are
dependent observations, not independent replications.
