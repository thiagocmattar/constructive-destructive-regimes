# Observation008: publication noise-sweep comparison

Figure: [sigma_comparison_paper.pdf](../figures/sigma_comparison_paper.pdf),
generated by [04_plot_sigma_paper.py](../04_plot_sigma_paper.py). This is a
presentation variant of the original sigma comparison for the formulation
report, with a compact serif three-row, two-column layout and shared LR colours.

Sources: all 15 frozen conditions in [cohort.json](../cohort.json), using
Runs038-040 as sigma-zero controls and Runs041-052 at nonzero sigma. History,
config and verification hashes are checked per condition. The 90 plotted scalars
match [results.json](../results.json) independently reduced from retained histories.
Original experiment inputs, reductions and all preceding PDFs are preserved.
No optimizer run, seed, cloud resource or new noise draw is introduced.

Caption: Gaussian gradient-estimation noise on Rosenbrock, sigma=0/0.01/0.1/1/10,
with the same three LRs, (0,1) start, 2,000 updates, AdamW decay0.1 and one paired
noise seed1234. (a) final exact objective; (b) median exact objective over states
1001-2000; (c,d) full-prefix B/A for noisy gradients and stored uncorrected
momentum; (e,f) means of unsmoothed adjacent cosines at updates1001-2000.
Objective Y is logarithmic, other Y axes linear. Sigma X uses symmetric log,
linear through0.01. Markers identify sampled conditions; lines are not a fit.

Reduction and coverage: each full-prefix B/A is norm(sum vectors)/sum norms,
retaining all 2,000 vectors. Late median and cosine means use 1,000 recorded
states or pairs, without averaging existing rolling curves, thinning or seed
averaging. There are 18 curves with five points each. All scalar values and
actual plotted X/Y arrays are checked. Noise is added to the analytic gradient,
not to the objective; sigma is an absolute coordinate standard deviation.

Findings and limits remain those of [Observation006](006-sigma-comparison.md):
in this paired realization final/late objective rises and raw B/A falls with
sigma. At sigma10 raw late mean alignment is close to zero while momentum stays
positive, despite higher objective. These are one-seed, finite-horizon patterns,
with no ensemble uncertainty, general noise law, stability or convergence claim.

The [publication manifest](../sigma_paper_manifest.json) records source,
scalar/plot-array and PDF hashes, original-PDF preservation, scales, dimensions,
and the inspected Poppler render. The [report builder](../../../manuscript/2026-10-11-restatement/00_build.py)
checks this evidence and embeds the figure after a brief experiment statement.
