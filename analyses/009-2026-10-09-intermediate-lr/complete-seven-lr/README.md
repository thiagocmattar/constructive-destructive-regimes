## Geometry reading

[Interpretation against the surgical manuscript proposal](observations/004-implications-for-manuscript.md) adds an exact aggregate audit from the recorded norms: whole-prefix raw cross-step interaction is positive at six rates and negative only atLR0.01, both with and without partial update712. The high-LR prefix first becomes negative at674; momentum/adaptive aggregate interaction remains positive. This retrospective contrast does not establish an early warning threshold. [Reproducible audit](interference_audit.json), generated by `../12_audit_interference.py`.

Within the four new runs, the best-loss condition has lower late adjacent momentum cosine than LR0.00215443 (0.852282 vs0.956273). All seven conditions have negative late raw cosine, with differing losses. High adaptive adjacent alignment also coexists with poor loss. These data do not establish a one-step geometric threshold for convergence, stability, noise variance or long-lag destructive interference.
