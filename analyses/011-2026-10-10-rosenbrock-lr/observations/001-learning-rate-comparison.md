# Observation001: loss navigation and raw/momentum geometry at three learning rates

Question H010: under one no-decay momentum setting, how do exact Rosenbrock
navigation and the requested collector diagnostics vary at LR 1e-2/1e-3/1e-4
with the same start and 2,000-update horizon? This is exploratory, with no
directional prediction or post-outcome LR selection.

Sources: [cohort](../cohort.json), frozen Runs032/033/034 configs and each
`artifacts/attempts/001-local/history.npz`. The exact source identities and
SHA-256 are in [figure_manifest.json](../figure_manifest.json); run inventories
also retain file sizes/hashes. [results.json](../results.json) records the
reduced values, [verification.json](../verification.json) full scientific
checks, and [figure_verification.json](../figure_verification.json) PDF checks.
No source history is truncated or overwritten. Scientific inputs and
interpretation limits are in [METHOD.md](../METHOD.md).

Reduction and coverage: all 2,001 positions and 2,000 transitions per condition,
both coordinates, float64, analytic objective gradients without clipping.
Loss is evaluated after each update, with the initial point included. Raw
g_t is evaluated at the previous point; m_t is the uncorrected first moment
after incorporating g_t. Norms are Euclidean. Cosines compare adjacent
vectors, with 1,999 defined pairs per stream and condition; update 1 is
undefined and omitted. Every defined cosine is an individual point, not a
connecting segment or smoothed statistic. Other series use every recorded
value. There are no sample weights, smoothing, thinning, stochastic seeds,
confidence bands or averaged curves. Coincident values can overlap visually.

For each stream z, A(t)=sum_(s<=t)||z_s|| and B(t)=||sum_(s<=t)z_s||. The
ratio is **B/A**, following the user's explicit correction before handoff,
and is directly computed with A as denominator. Zero A makes it undefined,
with no epsilon substitution; B=0 with positive A gives 0. All observed A
values here are positive; the first ratios equal 1. Norm/A-and-B panels
use log Y; cosine and ratio panels use linear Y. Prefix sums always start at
update 1, with no local windows or restarted accumulation.

Result: at equal update budget, LR 1e-2 reaches a much smaller objective and
the optimum's neighborhood, despite frequent negative adjacent raw/momentum
pairs and smaller cumulative B/A. Its best sampled loss is 2.74037e-9 at step
757, but its final gradient norm is 0.532236 and late loss keeps oscillating.
The two smaller LRs have monotonically decreasing sampled loss and positive
adjacent pairs during this horizon, with slower valley traversal. Their large
cumulative A and B magnitudes in panels f/g arise from accumulating larger vector
norms for longer along their own paths; they are not parameter displacement.
Momentum A is smaller than raw A at each final prefix, especially at LR 1e-2;
the actual step also involves bias correction and the second-moment preconditioner.
None first meets the joint f<=1e-8/gradient norm<=1e-4 criterion within these
2,000 updates, and none passes the final-100 sustained criterion.

Comparison with the earlier run: Run032's entire saved history equals the
first 2,000 updates of Run028, including the loss oscillations. LR, start,
betas, epsilon, decay and numerical method are unchanged for this matched
condition. The old [plotting script](../../010-2026-10-10-rosenbrock-controlled/02_plot.py)
uses trailing 100-update cosine means; current cosine panels show individual
pairs. The previous loss curve also uses every value, but occupies a smaller
panel over 10,000 updates. Its [late summary](../../../runs/028-2026-10-10-rosenbrock-adam/artifacts/attempts/001-local/summary.json)
has a last-1,000 loss range 2.21917e-9 to 1.42761e-4: oscillation continues
late in the old run as well. Thus the changed appearance is not a numerical
regression or a consequence of stopping earlier. The small-LR paths are at
different positions/optimization phases; absence of negative pairs so far
does not predict their behavior in the minimum's neighborhood.

Uncertainty and limits: deterministic trajectories have no sampling-error
estimate. Learning-rate interventions change paths and encountered gradients,
so alignment cannot be treated as the independently manipulated cause of loss
progress. B/A measures cumulative cancellation; its magnitude or slope does
not establish information destruction, stationarity or stability. One start,
objective, beta2 and finite horizon do not establish a universal optimizer/LR
ranking, asymptotic convergence or transfer to LLM training. This refinement
compares one method across LRs; it has no new no-momentum control.
