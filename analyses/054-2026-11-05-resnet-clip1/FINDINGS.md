# ResNet gradient clipping at 1

- The clipping and control branches each completed 320 full-objective updates on Spark from the same RTX 4090 state 256. The cross-GPU fork gradient differed by 0.0133%. Within-Spark two-update replay passed exactly, including Adam moments and RNG state.
- Clipping activated on 318 of 320 updates (99.4%). Its maximum raw gradient norm was 7.641 and its maximum Adam-input norm was 1.000000053, within FP32 rounding of the threshold. The maximum actual parameter-step norm was 0.158 versus 0.600 in the unclipped control.
- The first update raised training loss from 0.4826 to 0.5370 under clipping and lowered it to 0.4580 in the control. This first clipped step was also the clipped branch's highest loss in the response window.
- Clipping strongly reduced the two largest control spikes. Loss at step 382 was 0.222 clipped versus 0.956 control. At step 477 it was 0.119 versus 1.122. The Spark control peaked later than the RTX 4090 baseline, which is why the numerical treatment comparison uses the fresh Spark control.
- Mean loss over states 257 through 576 fell from 0.27171 to 0.19475 (28.3%). The maximum loss over these states fell by 52.2%.
- The same detector identified 26 excursions greater than 10% with clipping versus four without. The largest trough-to-peak ratio fell from 22.52 to 3.70. The last clipped excursion remained above its preceding trough at the endpoint. All four unclipped excursions recovered within the window.
- The unclipped model recovered from its large late spike and made faster progress during the final part of this window. Mean loss over states 545 through 576 was 0.04542 unclipped versus 0.08085 clipped. Endpoint loss was 0.03009 versus 0.07526. Thus lower peak and window-average loss coexisted with higher tail and endpoint loss under clipping.
- This comparison covers seed 0, ResNet-32 on the full 50,000-image CIFAR-10 objective, LR 0.003 and one warm checkpoint. Spark worker time was 2 h 44 min including qualification. All 38 new artifacts and 69 archived source files passed hash verification. CPU checkpoint checks confirmed final optimizer clocks of 576. The rendered vector PDF shows the hardware boundary and measured input norms.
