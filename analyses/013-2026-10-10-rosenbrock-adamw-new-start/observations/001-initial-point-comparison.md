# Observation001: Rosenbrock AdamW sensitivity to the initial point

H012 asks how loss navigation and raw-gradient/momentum geometry change under
the Analysis012 method when start (-1.2,1) becomes the user-selected (0,1).
Within the new cohort vary LR only; pair each new condition with its same-LR
previous run, changing initial point only. No directional hypothesis or
post-outcome change to scientific inputs is introduced.
[METHOD.md](../METHOD.md) records the pre-execution contract.

Runs035-037 from [Analysis012](../../012-2026-10-10-rosenbrock-adamw-lr/README.md).
[verification.json](../verification.json) records every config/source/history

AdamW updates, exact replay and all-stream identities. Prior run artifacts are
preserved without modification; this comparison has no matched no-decay control

Method: f(x,y)=(1-x)^2+100(y-x^2)^2, known optimum (1,1); 2,000 CPU float64
updates per condition, betas (0.9,0.95), epsilon 1e-8, zero moments, decay 0.1
on both coordinates, constant LR, full analytic gradients and no clipping.
Initial objective is 101 instead of 24.2; update budgets, not initial losses,
are matched. For update t, raw g_t is evaluated at theta_(t-1), stored m_t is
uncorrected first moment, and loss is measured at theta_t. Neither g nor m
contains decay; captured actual displacement does. Preserve adaptive directions,
second moments, complete positions and exact temporal terms as well.

Reduction/coverage: all 2,001 positions and 2,000 transitions per run contribute;
both coordinates and every update are measured. A=sum ||z_s|| and B=||sum z_s||
at every prefix, ratio B/A. First cosine/zero-norm adjacent pairs and A=0 ratios
are undefined; positive A with B=0 gives zero. Observed g/m each have 1,999
defined adjacent pairs, positive A throughout and 2,000 norms/prefixes. Table
statistics use complete unsmoothed histories and equal pair weighting; late
median cosines use the last 1,000 updates. No diagnostic epsilon or clipping.

At this start LR 1e-3 has the lowest final objective, whereas the midpoint
was lowest at the previous start. All three enter the optimum neighborhood;
the two larger LRs retain visibly fluctuating objective floors. The small LR
also has sampled loss increases (13.15% of updates); its low terminal loss is

The fixed-budget LR ordering is initialization-sensitive: the new 1e-3 path
reaches a much lower objective despite acquiring raw adjacent reversals;
the midpoint's final objective changes little. Adjacent momentum remains
mostly/entirely positive. High whole-prefix B/A can coexist with negative late
raw cosine means, as the early large vectors continue to contribute. B/A
cannot isolate the current local regime or prove a loss benefit. These are
descriptive comparisons of a starting-point intervention, not independent
manipulation of alignment.

Uncertainty/limits: only two deliberately selected deterministic starting
points of one function, recipe and horizon; there are no stochastic replicates
or confidence intervals. Changing the start changes initial loss, gradient
direction, coordinate shrinkage and the subsequent path. LR scales both
adaptive descent and decay. Finite terminal phase may affect final objective;
the complete last-1,000 loss ranges remain in results. No universal LR ranking,
asymptotic convergence/stability, causal alignment mechanism, generalization,
parameter-translation invariance or LLM transfer is established.
