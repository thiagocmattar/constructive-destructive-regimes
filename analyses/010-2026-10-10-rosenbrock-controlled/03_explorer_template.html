Definitions, coverage, and interpretation

f(x,y) = (1−x)² + 100(y−x²)². The known global minimum is (1,1), f=0. All conditions use exact analytic gradients without clipping, ε=10⁻⁸, and the same fixed 10,000-update budget. β₁ is 0 or 0.9; weight decay is 0 or 0.1.

At update t, g is evaluated at θt−1; m is the uncorrected first moment after incorporating g; d includes bias correction and second-moment division, excluding LR and decay; u=θt−θt−1 includes LR, decay, and descent sign. The selected point and loss are post-update. Adjacent cosine at t compares vectors t−1 and t. First-update cosine and zero denominators are undefined.

A=Σ‖z‖, B=‖Σz‖. A²−B² is a nonnegative triangle gap; signed all-pair interaction is I=B²−Σ‖z‖². Neither cosine sign nor B/A alone establishes loss decrease or convergence.

The declared convergence test requires f≤10⁻⁸ and post-update gradient norm≤10⁻⁴. Sustained convergence requires all final 100 updates to pass; no condition passes that test. One deterministic start, objective, LR, and β₂ do not establish an asymptotic or LLM result.

Curves and selected values use eight significant digits for display; no steps are omitted. Exact float64 histories, verification, hashes, and publication PDFs accompany the experiment in the repository. Full-domain contours use a 500×500 analytic mesh; transition zoom recomputes analytic contours on a 100×100 mesh with linearly interpolated crossings. Null diagnostics remain undefined. There are no network dependencies.
