# Figures and observations

Each figure belongs to its generating run or independent/cross-run analysis.
Keep the numbered plotting/reduction script, input identities, figure data, and
observation together. Publication output is PDF under `figures/`; preview renders
go in ignored scratch.

Every observation states the question, exact sources, coverage, filtering,
aggregation, caption/legend, result, uncertainty, limitations, and script/output
paths. Add it to the local `observations/INDEX.md`. A manuscript claim links to
that observation and generating analysis.

Label axes/units and report seeds/sample coverage. Use appropriate uncertainty
and disclose weighting; pooled ratios and averages of ratios have different
meanings. Use consistent scales across comparable panels and redundant
color/line/marker encodings. Display undefined quantities explicitly. Never
equate an alignment sign or norm trend with stability without supporting evidence.

Keep imported manuscript figures identified as legacy assets. New figure numbers
and claims must trace to this workspace's evidence, even if their layout reuses
an old figure.
